ARROW RUSH

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 July 2026

The short version

Arrow Rush is a puzzle game. We are not in the data business.

Your progress, coins, settings and any photo you pick stay on your phone.

We never ask for your name or email. There is no account and no sign-up.

We do not run analytics, and we do not track you across other apps.

The parts that do involve other companies are ads, the optional weekly leaderboard, notifications and purchases. Each one is explained below.

Who we are

Arrow Rush is published by NextInnovation ("we", "us"). The Android package name is com.arrowgame.app.

Any question about this policy or your data: johnsnowpatel19112000@gmail.com. A real person reads it.

What stays on your phone

This never leaves your device. We cannot see it and we hold no copy of it:

Your level progress and which levels you have unlocked

Your coins and whether you have removed ads

Your settings — skin, arrow style, sound, vibration, colour mode

Your daily-challenge streak, calendar and trophies

Any photo you choose to play as

If you uninstall the game, or use Settings then "Reset progress", this is gone for good. We cannot restore it, because we never had it.

Photos

If you choose to play as your own photo, the game asks permission to open your photo library.

The picture you pick is copied into the game's own private folder on your phone so it survives a restart. It is never uploaded, never shared, and never seen by us.

You can remove it at any time in Settings.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob — occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional "watch an ad" rewards that you choose to tap.

To serve them, Google's ad SDK receives information from your device, including an advertising identifier, your IP address (and therefore your rough location), your device type, and whether you interacted with the ad.

We request non-personalized ads only. The ads you see are not selected from a profile of your interests. Google may still process the information above to deliver ads, cap how often you see them, prevent fraud, and report in aggregate.

We never receive any of this ourselves — it goes to Google, not to us. Google explains its own handling at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

If you buy Remove Ads, the game stops requesting ads altogether.

Weekly leaderboard

The weekly tournament runs in one of two ways.

Offline, your rivals are generated on your own device and nothing is sent anywhere.

When the online leaderboard is switched on, the game sends only:

A random ID that the game made up on your device the first time it needed one. It is not your name, not your Google account, not your phone number, and not a hardware identifier.

Your tournament points for the week

A timestamp

A name field that is always the fixed text "You" — never your real name

That is the entire record. We do not send your name — your own row on the leaderboard literally reads "You". There is no account, and no way for us to link that random ID back to you as a person.

It is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore).

Want it removed? Email johnsnowpatel19112000@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Notifications

The game can send two kinds of notification. You can turn them off at any time in Settings, or in your phone's own system settings.

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself — for example, that your daily challenge is ready, or that your streak is about to break. No server is involved and no data leaves your device.

Messages from us — for example about a new event — need a push token. A push token identifies your installation of the game on your device and is issued by the notification service. We store it against the same random ID described above so we can send the message. The stored record is only:

The push token itself

Which platform to send it through — "android" or "ios"

A timestamp of when the token was registered

That is the entire record. It does not tell us who you are.

Turn notifications off and we stop sending. You can also ask us to delete your token: johnsnowpatel19112000@gmail.com

Purchases

If you buy something — Remove Ads, or coins — the purchase is handled by Google Play, and validated through RevenueCat.

We never see, receive, or store your card details. What comes back to us is essentially "this purchase is valid", plus a receipt identifier.

Refunds are handled by Google Play under their own policy.

Children

Arrow Rush is a general-audience game intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

If you believe a child has given us data, email johnsnowpatel19112000@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Push tokens are kept while the game is installed and notifications are on.

Anything on your device stays there until you delete it.
